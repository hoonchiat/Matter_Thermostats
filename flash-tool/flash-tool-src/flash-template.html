ESP32 Firmware Flasher

Flashes any of the project firmwares — Matter Hub (C6), Gateway Portal (S3), RGB Light (C6) and Button (C6) — over USB, straight from your browser. Firmware is embedded in this file — no downloads, works offline.
Giving a Light or Button a unique commissioning identity + a printable QR label? Use provisioning-tool.html instead — this flasher writes the same firmware (shared test identity) to every unit.
Web Serial not available. Open this file in desktop Chrome or Edge. Firefox, Safari, and mobile browsers can't talk to USB serial.
1 · Choose firmware
2 · Connect & verify chip
Plug the board into USB, click connect, and pick its serial port. I'll read the chip and confirm it matches your choice.
3 · Erase & flash
Full-chip erase, then writes the selected firmware. Don't unplug until it says done.

  


  
Tip: a factory-fresh ESP32-C6/S3 connects over its built-in USB (it shows up as a COM port). If the board doesn't respond, hold its BOOT button while connecting, then release.
