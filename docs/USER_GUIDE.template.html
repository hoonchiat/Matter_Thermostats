Overview

What it does

The thermostat keeps a room at your target temperature and appears in your smart-home app as a standard Matter thermostat over a Thread mesh — no cloud, all local. You can run it entirely from the dial and button, or from Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings or Home Assistant.

It supports two room-sensor builds: a 10 kΩ NTC (temperature only) or an SHT40 that also measures relative humidity. On the SHT40 build the humidity is shown on the home screen and published as a Matter humidity sensor.

Controls

The dial and the button

Two controls do everything: the rotary dial (turn and press) and the push button. A third button — the recessed BOOT — is used only for a factory reset.

First-time setup

Add it to your smart home

Out of the box the thermostat shows the Pairing screen and waits to be added. You'll need a Thread border router on your network — an Apple TV 4K or HomePod mini, a Google Nest Hub (2nd gen), an eero, or Home Assistant with a Thread radio.

  1. Power it on. The OLED shows a QR code and an 11-digit pairing code.
  2. Open your smart-home app and choose “Add device / Add Matter accessory”.
  3. Scan the QR code on the OLED, or type the pairing code shown beside it.
  4. Wait for it to join Thread. The app pushes your Thread network to the device; it joins the mesh and the home screen appears with a filled link dot.
  5. Done. Adjust it from the dial or the app — changes sync both ways.
🔒
No border router yet? The thermostat still works as a standalone dial — heating, cooling and fan all run locally. It simply won't appear in your app until a Thread border router is present.
Screens

The home screen

The resting screen shows the room temperature large in the middle, a status bar on top, the target in a rounded pill, and the current action along the bottom. It adapts to the mode, the fan, occupancy, units and whether an SHT40 is fitted.

Screens

Adjusting the temperature

Turn the dial and the screen switches to the large adjust view. Each click is 0.5°; a bar shows where you are within the allowed range. It commits and returns home a few seconds after you stop.

Screens

The settings menu

Long-press the button to open Settings. Turn the dial to move the highlight, press the dial to change the highlighted item, short-press the button to go back. The list scrolls, with a scrollbar on the right.

Screens

Pairing & the Matter code

Before it's added, the device shows the Pairing screen. After it's added, the same code is always available under Settings → Matter code so you can add it to another home without factory-resetting.

Screens

Sensor fault

If the temperature sensor reads open or shorted, the thermostat shows a fault screen and — for safety — switches all heating and cooling off until the sensor recovers. The status LED blinks red.
